1. What is the problem?

- The problem is about teaching Quantum Computing.

Quantum computing is difficult for beginners because concepts such as:

Qubits
Superposition
Entanglement
Quantum gates
Quantum algorithms

are quite abstract.

Existing learning resources are often:

📖 Mostly theoretical
📝 Static
🧠 Difficult to understand
🖥️ Not interactive
🔬 Don't provide enough practical experimentation

And students generally don't have access to real quantum computers.

So the problem statement wants us to create a platform where someone can learn quantum computing by actually experimenting with quantum circuits.

The official problem statement specifically asks for a platform combining theoretical instruction, visual circuit design, simulation, visualization, and AI-based guidance.

- The problem is real time simulation for quantum computing, and hands on experience for reserchers too, due to lack of hands on infrastructure.

So, the problem statement wants us to create a platform for quantum experimentation.
